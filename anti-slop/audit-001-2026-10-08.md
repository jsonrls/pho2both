## Detailed Findings & Proposed Remediations

### Finding 1: R-02 (Copywriting — No Em Dashes)
- **Priority**: HIGH (Hard Gate)
- **Locations**:
  - `frontend/src/app/layout.tsx:25` (`"Pho2Booth — A little booth for your best moments"`)
  - `frontend/src/components/LandingPage.tsx:82-84` (`"01 — Pick a layout"`, `"02 — Add a little color"`, `"03 — Set the mood"`)
  - `frontend/src/lib/constants.ts:185, 303, 383` (Preset descriptions)
- **Remediation**: Replace all em dashes (`—`) with natural colons, commas, or clean sentence formatting (e.g. `"Pho2Booth: A photo booth for your favorite moments"`, `"1. Pick a layout"`).

### Finding 2: R-03 (Mobile Responsiveness — 44px Minimum Tap Targets)
- **Priority**: HIGH (Hard Gate)
- **Locations**:
  - `frontend/src/components/StripCustomizer.tsx:1181, 1189`
  - `frontend/src/components/SinglePhotoStudio.tsx:224`
  - `frontend/src/components/AiEffectStudio.tsx:199`
  - `frontend/src/components/Studios.module.css:31`
- **Remediation**: Ensure all interactive controls and secondary studio buttons meet the 44px minimum tap target height and comfortable padding for touch devices.

### Finding 3: R-24 (Navigation — Destination Integrity)
- **Priority**: HIGH (Hard Gate)
- **Locations**: `frontend/src/components/SiteHeader.tsx:24`, `LandingPage.tsx:95`
- **Remediation**: Replace anchor jump `#how-it-works` in the header with a valid route or contextual link that resolves accurately from every page.

### Finding 4: R-28 (FAQ — Remove Generic Template FAQ)
- **Priority**: HIGH (Hard Gate)
- **Locations**: `frontend/src/components/LandingPage.tsx:100-105`
- **Remediation**: Remove generic boilerplate FAQ accordion ("Do I need an app?", "Can I use it without a camera?") and integrate clear, concise helper copy directly where users need it (camera permissions & photo upload card).

### Finding 5: R-04 (Icons — Remove AI Sparkles & Decorative Glyphs)
- **Priority**: MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate)
- **Locations**:
  - `frontend/src/components/AiEffectStudio.tsx:265` (`<Sparkles />`)
  - `frontend/src/components/StripCustomizer.tsx:819` (`<Sparkles />`)
  - `frontend/src/components/FormatSelectionScreen.tsx:25` (`<Sparkles />`)
  - `frontend/src/components/LandingPage.tsx:64, 68, 106` (`<Star />`, `✳`)
- **Remediation**: Remove decorative AI sparkles, star SVGs, and asterisk glyphs. Use functional photography iconography (e.g. contrast/sliders for creative effects) or let the typography and photo art speak for themselves.

### Finding 6: R-08 (Button Arrows — Eliminate Gratuitous Arrow Decoration)
- **Priority**: MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate)
- **Locations**: Buttons and links in `SiteHeader.tsx`, `LandingPage.tsx`, `CameraView.tsx`, `FormatSelectionScreen.tsx`, `PhotoPreviewScreen.tsx`, `download/page.tsx`, `auth/page.tsx`.
- **Remediation**: Strip out tacked-on `<ArrowRight />` and `↗` glyphs from buttons and links unless strictly required for multi-step directional progression.

### Finding 7: R-09 (Badges — Remove Monotonous Eyebrow Pills Above Headlines)
- **Priority**: MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate)
- **Locations**:
  - `frontend/src/app/globals.css:61` (`.eyebrow, .bp-eyebrow`)
  - `LandingPage.tsx`, `CameraView.tsx`, `FormatSelectionScreen.tsx`, `PhotoPreviewScreen.tsx`, `SinglePhotoStudio.tsx`, `AiEffectStudio.tsx`, `download/page.tsx`, `auth/page.tsx`, `Library.tsx`
- **Remediation**: Remove formulaic uppercase category pill tags parked above headlines. Structure headings with clean typographic hierarchy and honest editorial subtitles.

### Finding 8: R-05 (Page Structure — Break AI 3-Step Template & Uniform Rhythm)
- **Priority**: LOW (Quality Lock)
- **Locations**: `frontend/src/components/LandingPage.tsx:95-99`
- **Remediation**: Replace the boilerplate 3-step card grid with an organic preview layout highlighting authentic photo strip keepsakes, prints, and formats.

### Finding 9: R-11 (Border Radius — Cohesive Design System Scale)
- **Priority**: LOW (Quality Lock)
- **Locations**: `frontend/src/app/globals.css:18`, studio layout containers in `StripCustomizer.tsx`
- **Remediation**: Standardize radii across the app to a consistent 2-tier system: subtle 8px for inner controls/inputs and 14px for cards/containers, avoiding random pill and extreme radius stacking.

### Finding 10: R-16 (Copywriting — Replace AI Marketing Jargon)
- **Priority**: LOW (Quality Lock)
- **Locations**: `FormatSelectionScreen.tsx:18`, `AiEffectStudio.tsx`, `auth/page.tsx:48`
- **Remediation**: Replace "Creative effects (AI)" with "Darkroom & Stylized Filters", and "Send magic link" with "Send sign-in link".

### Finding 11: R-15 (CTAs — Purposeful & Distinct Action Copy)
- **Priority**: LOW (Quality Lock)
- **Locations**: `LandingPage.tsx:57, 106`
- **Remediation**: Consolidate hero and section CTAs into distinct, user-centered actions ("Open the booth", "Upload photos").

### Finding 12: R-29 (Color Palette — Restrained Paper, Ink & Tomato Accent)
- **Priority**: LOW (Quality Lock)
- **Locations**: `frontend/src/app/globals.css:4-19`
- **Remediation**: Refine color system into an authentic tactile palette: warm paper background (`#f7f4eb`), deep ink typography (`#29251f`), and signature tomato red (`#d94c35` / `#ea6c58`) as the deliberate focal accent.
